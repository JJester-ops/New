## Three storylines

### A. "The $8,887 receipt" — recommended
- Device / form: a number lands · one frame that rebuilds.
- First 2 s: a receipt slams in reading `$5,000 BALANCE · MINIMUM PAYMENTS · INTEREST: $8,887`. VO: "Paying the minimum on $5,000 costs you $8,887."
- Beats: hook → a timeline bar crawls to 19.5 YEARS → the payment field changes $150 → $250 → bar collapses to 26 MONTHS, interest drops to $1,449 → "Pay a fixed amount, never the minimum" → end card "Comment PLAN".
- Look: near-black ground, receipt-paper white, interest in red, savings in green, heavy condensed type.
- Sound: VO, low pulse bed, receipt-printer and cash-register hits on each number.

### B. "The debt race"
- Device / form: life before and after · split screen to one.
- First 2 s: two lanes labelled ❄️ SNOWBALL and 🏔️ AVALANCHE, same three cards, a starting gun. VO: "Same debt, same paycheck. One finishes first."
- Beats: hook → snowball clears the small card first (first win) → avalanche pulls ahead on interest → finish line: who saved more, who quit less → "Pick the one you'll stick to" → end card.
- Look: race-track lanes, card balances as runners. Numbers computed before building.

### C. "0% — what's the catch?"
- Device / form: a question with the input · match cuts (the % sign holds while everything changes).
- First 2 s: a giant "24%" with an interest counter ticking +$100, then it flips to "0%". VO: "This card charges 0% for 18 months. Here's the catch."
- Beats: hook → same $286/mo: 22 months and $1,214 interest at 24% vs 18 months and a $150 fee at 0% → the catches (3–5% fee, needs good credit, rate jumps when the promo ends) → end card.
- Compliance: affiliate disclosure in description; "not financial advice".
